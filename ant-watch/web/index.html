How the states are decided
  • Rising: trend strength (ADX) ≥ 25, buyers ahead (+DI above −DI), price above the trend line and the fast EMA above the slow EMA.
  • Falling: the mirror image of Rising.
  • Volatile: no clear trend, and the Bollinger band is wider than 80% of recent history, or the typical move (ATR) is at least 1.5× its usual size.
  • Stable: ADX under 20, Bollinger band narrower than 65% of recent history and ATR not elevated.
  • Normal: everything else.
  • A new state has to show up on 2 bars in a row before it counts, which stops it flip-flopping.
  • It uses 15-minute bars while the stock is new, then switches to daily bars after 60 trading days.

Each stock has its own GitHub dashboard issue, which sends the alerts. Prices are from Yahoo Finance and can be delayed by up to 15 minutes.
Indicators describe the past, not the future. This is not financial advice.
